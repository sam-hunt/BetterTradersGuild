Better Traders Guild

[h1]About[/h1]
The Traders Guild are the faction behind orbital trader events in RimWorld's Odyssey DLC. This mod expands their presence with peaceful trading, richer settlement interiors, living inhabitants, and new ways to interact with them.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]Peaceful Trading[/h2]
[list]
[*]Visit the Traders Guild on the world map via shuttle when relations are Neutral or better
[*]Settlements cycle through orbital trader types on a configurable schedule
[*]Preview which trader is docked on the world map and plan your trips around the planet!
[*]Orbital trade requests - Guild settlements send shuttle delivery jobs with goodwill and silver rewards
[/list]

[h2]Overhauled Map Generation[/h2]
[list]
[*]New room types (Control Center, Crew Quarters, Cargo Hold, Commander's Lounge, Medbay, and more)
[*]Cargo vault items spawn from trade inventory - steal it and it's gone from their world-layer trade stock
[*]Lived-in aesthetics befitting wealthy merchants
[/list]

[h2]Living Settlements[/h2]
[list]
[*]Entrenched garrisons defend their home instead of charging into vacuum: they hold the structure, eat, sleep, tend to wounds, and call in resupply drops (and reinforcements) during long sieges
[*]Worker mechs go about their jobs: paramedics rescue and treat casualties, cleansweepers keep the halls clean, agrihands farm the greenhouse
[*]Civilians take shelter during assault and make a break for the shuttle if the shelter is breached
[*]Break a settlement by crippling its security force; survivors abandon ship rather than fighting to the death
[/list]

[h2]Smuggler's Den Quest[/h2]
[list]
[*]Experience the custom BTG assault map generation, even if you're committed to friendly relations, or prefer a different mod's map generation for Traders Guild settlements (e.g. VGE2).
[*]Choose your reward: Faction goodwill, or claim orbital trader cargo vault goods for yourself
[/list]

[h2]New Scenarios[/h2]
[olist]
[*][b]Independent Traders[/b] - Leave the Guild to strike out on your own. Start with a shuttle, silver, and allied standing with your former employers.
[*][b]Exiled Traders[/b] - A salvage job gone wrong awakens a mechhive. The Guild cuts you loose. Scavenge what you can and survive with mechanoids on your tail. Not for the faint of heart.
[/olist]
Reusable scenario parts: shuttle arrivals, unique weapons with traits, apparel coloring, faction goodwill, and xenotype pawn slot configuration

[h1]FAQ[/h1]
[b]Q: Do I need the Odyssey DLC?[/b]
A: Yes. This mod enhances Odyssey's Traders Guild faction.

[b]Q: Do I still need a signal jammer to attack them?[/b]
A: Yes.

[b]Q: Does this make the game easier?[/b]
A: Possibly! It adds more trading opportunities. Guild settlements have much higher wealth but are also better defended.

[b]Q: Trader stock rotation/map generation/cargo vault/defender behavior/quest frequency is imbalanced![/b]
A: All configurable in mod settings.

[b]Q: Why isn't a specific trader type showing up?[/b]
A: Trader types are selected randomly based on commonality values. Some are rarer than others. Wait for rotations or check other settlements. Commonality of trader types from other mods might not be balanced.
